/PRIVACY[ DRAFT · CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH ]

Short and plain.

PowerSave works without an account. Here is what is kept, and where.

/01 GUESTS
Only on this device.

Your plan, chores and preferences are saved in your browser. They never leave your device. Clear your browser data and they're gone.

/02 SIGNED-IN USERS
Email, plan and chores.

If you choose to sign in, we store your email address, your plan and your chores so they follow you across devices. That's all.

/03 SELLING
We don't sell your data.

Not your email, not your plan, not your chores. There are no ads in PowerSave.

/04 DELETING
One button.

Settings has a "Delete my data" button. It clears what's on this device and, if you signed in, your account.
